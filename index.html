01 — About me

Strategist by craft. Storyteller by instinct.

A snapshot of who I am — the work that drives me, the people who anchor me, and the curiosities that keep me learning every single day.

I'm Rahul Bhushan — a Digital Strategy, Transformation, and Organization Design leader, currently serving as Global Director, Organization Design, GBS & Mirai Transformation at Unilever. With more than 20 years of experience across consulting and industry, I've helped organizations reimagine how they operate by aligning strategy, people, process, and technology to drive sustainable value creation.

My career has spanned leadership roles across Financial Services, Telecoms, FMCG, and Consulting, working with organizations including ICICI Bank, Ernst & Young, Vodafone, Adobe, and now Unilever. Over the years, I've designed large-scale operating models, led enterprise-wide technology and workforce transformations, governed multi-billion-pound workforce budgets, established greenfield delivery centers at speed, and delivered SAP SuccessFactors implementations across 29 markets.

I've also led large-scale digital marketing and customer experience transformations focused on Customer Data Platforms (CDPs), AI-driven content transformation, and digital commerce — helping organizations modernize their marketing ecosystems, unlock data-driven personalization, and scale digital engagement globally.

I'm passionate about building organizations that are not only more efficient and scalable, but also more human-centered, resilient, and future-ready.

"Aim for the stars — you may reach the moon, and then aim even higher."

Outside of work, I'm a husband and father to two daughters — the people who keep me grounded and remind me what truly matters. I'm also an amateur wildlife photographer, a passionate home baker, an avid reader of history and management, and a foodie who travels as much for cuisine as for culture.

06 — Leadership philosophy

How I build & lead teams

Three pillars on the foundation of data-driven decision-making — supported by the principle that the world is one family.

"
Aim for the stars — you will at least get the moon. Then aim again. Play to win, play hard, play fair. Live your values.— My personal operating principle
01
Best-in-class talent
Absolutely no compromises in selection. Nurture talent, build growth mindsets, and develop succession depth at every level.
02
Value-driven excellence
Customer first. Create the future. Own outcomes. Play to win, play hard, play fair — and live your values.
03
Trust & psychological safety
A culture of open debate where argumentation and challenging the status quo are expected. Belonging is the soil where performance grows.
04
Data to cut the clutter
Use data to simplify, not complicate. Evidence over opinion — whether sizing a transformation or structuring a team.

"I lead by creating an unbossed environment — giving people decision-making authority within a strategic framework, then removing blockers and ensuring alignment so they truly own outcomes."

On empowering teams · Malaga setup

"I make curiosity a habit. We hold regular innovation sessions where the only goal is to question what we are doing — no deliverable pressure, just space to think differently."

On building a learning culture

"Psychological safety is the top priority. When someone raises a concern, I thank them publicly — that one act signals more about culture than any policy document ever could."
